# Confluence

**Confluence** is the selected name and production identity for the portfolio’s non-letter symbol. **Three Currents** is the descriptive model behind it.

The symbol represents three enduring threads meeting within one personal point of view. It contains no letters and is not a coded monogram. The written identity remains **Md Imrul Hassan** everywhere the name is shown.

## The three currents

- **Deep teal — `#0F5F59`** represents engineering and software: the structural foundation.
- **Mint — `#8FC1B4`** represents research, curiosity and observation: the exploratory current.
- **Coral — `#D98462`** represents music, poetry and photography: creative practice and human warmth.

The paths cross away from the centre to suggest interaction without assigning a hierarchy. They are related modes of attention rather than a literal career chronology.

## The frame

The enclosing shape represents focus and coherence: different interests held within one considered point of view. It can also suggest a window or photographic frame, but should not read as confinement or as a literal camera icon.

## Supporting colours

- **Warm paper — `#F7F4EF`** provides a calm, personal editorial ground.
- **Charcoal — `#172421`** provides text clarity and a stable dark-context colour.

Together, the palette is called **Quiet Curiosity**. Deep teal is the principal identity colour; mint and coral support it rather than compete with it.

## Usage guidance

- Preserve broad curves, decisive contrast and open negative space at 16px.
- Keep the crossing off-centre and the frame substantial enough to retain its silhouette.
- Do not add labels, initials, hobby icons, gradients or additional colours.
- Avoid turning the curves into roads, Wi-Fi bars, an audio waveform or a compass.
- Pair the symbol with the complete name **Md Imrul Hassan** in wordmarks and identity contexts.
- The mark must work visually without requiring this explanation. The story supports recognition; it does not compensate for unclear geometry.
