I’m **Md Imrul Hassan**. Most people call me Imrul. I’m a software engineer with roots in electrical engineering and wireless-network research, and a lasting interest in making things outside those disciplines.

## From radio signals to software

I studied Electrical and Electronic Engineering at IUT, completed a postgraduate diploma at BUET and a master’s in Radio Engineering at Kyung Hee, then earned my PhD at Swinburne. My research explored the reliability of wireless communication for vehicle safety.

After teaching and research, I moved into professional software development in 2015 and joined Google in 2016. Today I work as a senior software engineer, with work connected to Maps. My independent projects are a separate space for trying ideas, tools and languages.

[Read the career story](/career/) or [explore the research](/research/).

## A few things I return to

These are recurring themes in my work and interests, not a personality test.

### Understanding systems

I like finding the structure beneath a complicated behaviour, then testing whether the model holds up. That connects my [wireless-network research](/research/) to how I think about software.

### Staying with a puzzle

Rubik’s Cube, word games, competitive programming and tiling problems have kept me interested for years. [Q-Less Solver](/work/qless-solver/) is one way that curiosity became a tool.

### Making something useful

I enjoy small, well-defined products: a solver, a pronunciation tool, a direction finder. [My projects](/work/) tend to begin with a concrete question rather than a grand platform idea.

### Learning through languages

Haskell is a particularly persistent interest. Kotlin, TypeScript, Dart, Ruby and Python offer other ways to express a problem. [GitHub](https://github.com/mihassan) is where many of those experiments live.

### Leaving room for a creative voice

I write poems in Bangla and record songs, including several original compositions. Music and writing give me ways to explore experience that are different from engineering. [Visit the creative collection](/creative/).

### Looking more closely

Photography, nature and travel are reasons to slow down. Birds, flowers, landscapes and cities have all found their way into [my photographs](https://www.flickr.com/people/mihassan).
