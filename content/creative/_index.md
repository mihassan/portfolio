Some things are easier to understand by making them: singing a song, writing a line, or waiting for the light. A melody, a poem, and a photograph offer different ways to notice and express something—not every question needs the same kind of answer.

## Music

I record mostly Bangla and subcontinental songs, drawing from modern and film music, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, folk and ghazal. Most recordings are **covers**, not songs I wrote.

My original compositions include **Kotodin Brishti Viji Na**, **Ekhono Asheni Dak** and **Kal Shararat Ghum Ashe Ni**. Some songs have more than one recorded version.

[Listen on SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/mdimrulhassan). I also have an [earlier, smaller account](https://soundcloud.com/mdimrul-hassan) with overlapping material.
