Some things are easier to understand by making them: singing a song, writing a line, or waiting for the light. These practices have stayed with me alongside engineering.

## Music

I record mostly Bangla and subcontinental songs, drawing from modern and film music, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, folk and ghazal. Most recordings are **covers**, not songs I wrote.

My original compositions include **Kotodin Brishti Viji Na**, **Ekhono Asheni Dak** and **Kal Shararat Ghum Ashe Ni**, as identified in their recording descriptions. Some songs have more than one recorded version.

[Listen on SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/mdimrulhassan). I also have an [earlier, smaller account](https://soundcloud.com/mdimrul-hassan) with overlapping material. Recordings stay on their original platforms rather than being copied here.

### Words and melodies in other voices

**[Kal Sara Raat — কাল সারা রাত](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcOinm_fPwE)**

I wrote the lyrics and tune. Parvin Sultana sings the song, with music credited to Jk Majlish. The official video is published by SevenTunes Entertainment, whose description credits me as **Md Imrul Hassan** for both lyrics and tune. The recording and video remain on the label's YouTube channel.

**[মায়ার বাঁধন](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7d8lD5TAwSg)**

My poem, set to music and performed by Sazal Roy. His video description credits me for the poem and identifies the tune and singing as his own. [Read the poem](/poems/mayar-badhon/).

## Writing

Twelve poems in Bangla, about memory, longing, rain and the pull of the natural world. Ten were shared directly for this collection; **জীর্ণ স্মৃতি** and **বেদনার রঙ** retain the texts from my earlier website.

[Browse all twelve poems](/poems/), including [কাল জোছনা](/poems/kal-jochona/), [ঘুমকন্যা](/poems/ghumkonnya/) and [চল চলে যাই](/poems/chol-chole-jai/).

My [Medium archive](https://medium.com/@mihassan) also holds older technical notes and writing, including *Linux One Liners*, *Personality Traits*, *কাল জোছনা* and *Mind Hacking*. The initials of the lines in *Mind Hacking* spell IMRUL HASSAN—an acrostic in the original poem, not a replacement for my full name.

## Photography

I have photographed birds, flowers, landscapes, cities and night skies. An earlier version of this website held a small gallery; my older photographs include work made with a Canon EOS 450D.

For now, the photographs remain in [my Flickr archive](https://www.flickr.com/people/mihassan) and on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mihassan/). The abstract illustrations on this site are newly created artwork, not substitutes presented as my photographs.
