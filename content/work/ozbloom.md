**OzBloom** is a project for discovering Australian native flowers. Its subject is something I also enjoy through photography: noticing the detail in the natural world.

## A reason to look closer

Software can be an invitation to pay attention outdoors. A name or a visual reference gives a familiar-looking flower a little more context, and makes the next walk more interesting.

This project brings together that curiosity about nature with the pleasure of making a small visual tool.

## A note on imagery

Illustrative or generated botanical imagery is not a substitute for authoritative identification. The abstract artwork used on this portfolio is decorative, not a photograph or a botanical specimen.
